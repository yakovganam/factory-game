## Identities

- **Toy & Robot Workshop** (`toy_workshop`): Imported parts become block sets, toy cars and small toy robots; balance bench, paint and packing stations across modular product variants.
- **Sawmill** (`sawmill`): Logs are broken down by selectable cutting layouts that trade lumber and beam yield against recoverable offcuts, which are chopped into wood pieces for the toy workshop.
- **Engineered Wood Plant** (`engineered_wood_plant`): Veneers, chips and lumber are glued and hot-pressed in batches; plywood, particleboard and glulam beams compete for the same limited press time.
- **Furniture Factory** (`furniture_factory`): Panels, dried lumber and fittings flow through configurable assembly routes — flat-pack or pre-assembled, plain or upholstered — trading freight volume against showroom value.
- **Paper Mill** (`paper_mill`): Chips and recovered paper are pulped with heavy water use, then the drying section’s capacity decides how much kraft, paperboard and tissue the mill can finish.
- **Packaging Factory** (`packaging_factory`): Paperboard and kraft become flat cartons, shipping boxes, bags, pallets and trays; package dimensions decide how efficiently every downstream product ships.
- **Textile Mill** (`textile_mill`): Cotton and polyester are spun, woven or knitted, then dyed in colour batches; every machine setup and colour change costs hours, so runs must be sequenced light to dark.
- **Clothing Factory** (`clothing_factory`): Marker layouts set how much fabric becomes scraps, and stitching lines are balanced across t-shirt, denim, hoodie and bed-linen ranges.
- **Footwear Factory** (`footwear_factory`): Uppers and soles must meet as matched pairs on the lasting machine, and the curing oven is the bottleneck that sets daily pair output.
- **Glassworks** (`glassworks`): A continuously running furnace must be fed a sand or cullet batch and drained into panes, bottles or jars, while lehr cooling capacity limits how fast glassware can be finished.
- **Steelworks** (`steelworks`): Choose the input mix — ore through the blast furnace or scrap through the arc furnace — then move hot metal through rolling stages and ship bulk sheet, beams and rebar.
- **Aluminum Plant** (`aluminum_plant`): Primary smelting burns enormous power for light sheet, profiles and cans, so recovering scrap aluminum at a fraction of the energy is the key margin decision.
- **Wire & Cable Factory** (`cable_factory`): Copper is cast, drawn and insulated to different cable specifications, and limited spool capacity forces long runs of one spec before switching.
- **Plastics Factory** (`plastics_factory`): Pellets feed injection molds for toy parts, wheels and housings, film and bottles; every mold change costs time, and sprues are reground into pellets.
- **Tire & Rubber Factory** (`tire_factory`): Green tires queue for a limited set of curing presses, so mold queues for car, bike and solid tires must be balanced against long curing batches.
- **Concrete Plant** (`concrete_plant`): Ready-mix concrete starts setting the moment it is batched, so batches must be timed to construction deliveries while precast panels and blocks absorb spare capacity.
- **Brick & Tile Works** (`brick_works`): One tunnel kiln is loaded with bricks, roof tiles or glazed ceramic tiles; what goes on each kiln car sets the week’s product mix and fuel bill.
- **Paint & Coatings Plant** (`paint_plant`): Resins, pigments and coatings share mixing vessels, so recipe order and clean changeovers decide whether batches stay contamination-free.
- **Precision Parts Factory** (`precision_parts_factory`): Lathes, mills, grinders and presses are specialised by tooling; tighter tolerances raise value but cut throughput and send more steel to scrap.
- **Electric Motor Factory** (`motor_factory`): Stators are wound, rotors pressed and motors assembled, then every industrial motor runs a performance test that can reject weak units.
- **Grain Mill** (`grain_mill`): Wheat is milled at a chosen extraction rate — white flour, wholemeal or semolina, with more or less bran — while silos keep grain grades segregated.
- **Industrial Bakery** (`industrial_bakery`): Dough batches are scheduled through a shared tunnel oven; every bake starts a freshness clock, so the oven plan must follow same-day demand.
- **Pasta Factory** (`pasta_factory`): Semolina dough is shaped through interchangeable dies — long, short or filled — and the drying rooms cap how much dry pasta can leave each shift.
- **Dairy Plant** (`dairy_plant`): Raw milk must be separated, pasteurised and filled before it spoils; the cold chain and shelf life decide how much becomes milk, butter, yogurt or long-life powder.
- **Cheese Factory** (`cheese_factory`): Curd is pressed into wheels and aging-room space must be split between quick young cheese and slow, valuable matured cheese.
- **Beverage Factory** (`beverage_factory`): Juice pressing and syrup mixing must stay synchronised with the bottling and canning lines, which stall the moment a tank or a container feed runs dry.
- **Canning Factory** (`canning_factory`): Seasonal tomato, vegetable and fruit gluts are packed into cans and jars and sterilised in large retort batches that must be filled before they run.
- **Frozen Food Factory** (`frozen_food_factory`): Freezer throughput is the bottleneck: pizzas, fries and vegetables queue for the spiral freezer and must leave in refrigerated trucks without breaking the cold chain.
- **Chocolate Factory** (`chocolate_factory`): Imported cocoa is roasted and conched into dark or milk chocolate, then tempered into bars or hand-decorated praline boxes for a premium range.
- **Snack Factory** (`snack_factory`): One fryer feeds several seasoning variants, and the bagging line’s speed decides whether salted, cheese or cracker lines keep up with demand.
- **Circuit Board Factory** (`pcb_factory`): Boards are laminated, etched and routed through pick-and-place lines, then optical inspection rejects faulty mainboards and phone boards before they reach assembly.
- **Semiconductor Plant** (`semiconductor_plant`): Imported wafers pass abstracted patterning, deposition and probe stages in clean conditions; yield and cleanroom discipline decide how many chips each wafer delivers.
- **Battery Factory** (`battery_factory`): Cells spend a long formation time on charge racks, then testing sorts them into grade-A cells for packs and grade-B cells for small devices.
- **Computer Factory** (`computer_factory`): Configurable specifications — budget plastic laptops, aluminium pro laptops and desktops — share one assembly line, and test stations cap how many systems ship per hour.
- **Smartphone Factory** (`smartphone_factory`): Basic and pro model variants share a precision assembly cell; tight tolerances on housings and display bonding decide yield and margin.
- **Wearables Factory** (`wearables_factory`): Compact assembly packs sensors, cells and screens into tiny cases; every added feature trades battery life and yield against a higher price tier.
- **Audio Equipment Factory** (`audio_factory`): Drivers are built in-house and every product is tuned in an anechoic booth; booth time is split between earbuds, portable speakers and premium hi-fi pairs.
- **Home Appliance Factory** (`appliance_factory`): Bulky fridges, washers and microwaves need floor space, pallets and long test cycles; better motors and gaskets raise energy ratings at higher cost.
- **Camera Factory** (`camera_factory`): Lenses are ground and bonded to sensors, and every module needs calibration time; specification choices route modules to phones, drones, security or pro cameras.
- **Network Hardware Factory** (`network_factory`): Routers and switches are configured per order and must survive long burn-in on test racks before shipping, so rack space sets output.
- **Industrial Robot Plant** (`robotics_factory`): Servo actuators, arm frames and safety controllers are integrated into six-axis industrial arms, then calibrated with a picking or palletizing task module for logistics customers.
- **Drone Factory** (`drone_factory`): Payload and range trade off through motor and battery count, and every drone must be scheduled into the netted flight-test cage before it ships.
- **Bicycle Factory** (`bicycle_factory`): Frames, wheelsets and drivetrains meet at assembly stations, where each order can be customised into a city, mountain or cargo e-bike.
- **E-Scooter Factory** (`escooter_factory`): Standard and long-range scooters compete for battery packs and charge-test bays, so the product balance follows both demand and cell supply.
- **Electric Vehicle Factory** (`ev_factory`): Body, paint and chassis lines run in parallel and must stay synchronised so painted bodies and battery chassis meet at final assembly in the same takt.
- **Forklift Factory** (`forklift_factory`): Masts, counterweights and electric drives become forklifts and reach trucks that must pass load testing; they raise warehouse productivity when delivered.
- **Solar Panel Factory** (`solar_factory`): Cells are graded by efficiency and the module layout decides whether top-grade strings become premium panels or mixed cells become standard ones.
- **LED Lighting Factory** (`led_factory`): LED dies are binned by efficiency and brightness; high bins become panel lights and streetlights with strict illumination specs, low bins become household bulbs.
- **Medical Device Factory** (`medical_factory`): Fictional diagnostic monitors and ultrasound scanners are built in traceable batches, and strict QA release checks reject any unit that fails.
- **Modular Building Factory** (`modular_factory`): Wall panels, floor cassettes and bathroom pods are combined into room modules for large construction orders, timed to the construction company’s site schedule.
- **Supermarket** (`supermarket`): Allocates shelf, chiller and freezer space across city-made groceries; restocking, freshness and checkout queues decide what residents actually buy.
- **Electronics Store** (`electronics_store`): Chooses an assortment of city-made devices and appliances; limited display space and fast-moving trends decide which models earn a counter.
- **Toy Store** (`toy_store`): Sells boxed toys to residents and visitors; shelf space, pricing and weekly toy trends decide which lines to stock.
- **Furniture Showroom** (`furniture_showroom`): Displays furniture room sets; floor space per line and the home-delivery schedule decide what sells.
- **Café** (`cafe`): Turns city-made bread, cheese, pasta and milk into meals and coffee; menu choice, freshness and service speed set the queue of diners.
- **Hotel** (`hotel`): Sells guest nights and breakfasts; furnishing quality, linen and food supply set service quality, which drives occupancy.
- **Delivery Depot** (`delivery_depot`): Sorts parcels onto delivery routes; van, cargo-bike and drone capacity per route decides how fast online orders reach homes.
- **Distribution Warehouse** (`distribution_warehouse`): Receives, stores and dispatches city freight; slotting and dispatch policies (first-in-first-out, priority, cross-dock) set how much it can buffer.
- **Fulfillment Center** (`fulfillment_center`): Sells city goods online, then picks, batches and packs each order into parcels for the delivery depot.
- **Construction Company** (`construction_company`): Consumes city-made bricks, concrete, steel, glass and later modular units to complete expansion projects that grow the city.
